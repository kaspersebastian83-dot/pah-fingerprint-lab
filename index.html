03 / Private by design

Runs entirely on your computer

No account, server, dependencies or internet requests. Imported CSV stays in memory until the page closes or you load a new dataset. Avoid uploading confidential case data to public repositories.

CSV rules

Required metadata: sample_id, group, sample_type, unit and the 16 analyte columns.

Accepted value formats

4.5 = quantified; <0.5 = below LOQ of 0.5; blank = missing, not zero. Semicolon CSV with decimal commas also works (e.g. <0,5). Use wipe / material and units µg/m², µg/100cm², or mg/kg TM (DM accepted). For wipes, 1 µg/100 cm² = 100 µg/m².

09 / Scientific guardrails

What this app can and cannot tell you

  • No automatic source attribution. Even visually close profiles and diagnostic ratios cannot by themselves establish whether material came from wood, plastic, traffic or cooking.
  • Matrix matters. Wipe loading (µg/m²) and bulk material content (mg/kg dry matter) are not comparable. Wipe-area units are converted mathematically, but sampling procedures, substrates and surface conditions still matter.
  • Censored ≠ zero. An entry such as <0.5 means the value lies below the specified quantification limit, not that the concentration is zero. The slider is only an exploratory sensitivity check. A complete dataset is required for similarity and PCA.
  • Σ16 ranges. The displayed interval adds quantified values and 0–LOQ for censored compounds. Missing analytes mean the full Σ16 upper bound is unknown. Analytical uncertainty is not included.
  • PCA is exploratory. It is computed on linear normalized fractions, not log-ratio transformed compositions; shape and group separation can change with treatment of censored observations and sampling design. Do not interpret distances as independent proof of causation.
  • Validation. The CSV reader validates structure and numerical input but cannot verify whether a value was copied correctly from an original lab report.
